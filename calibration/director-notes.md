## 2. Universal Motion Design Principles & Directorial Intent

1. **Intentional Snaps vs. Drifting**:
   - Snaps on sound design accents, voiceover pauses, or UI state triggers (e.g. sharp camera pans, rapid pushes, or spring rebounds) are **essential artistic accents**. They give motion authority and commercial punch. They must never be artificially smoothed out into sluggish, floaty drift to appease a naive smoothness curve.
2. **Intentional Negative Space & Blackout Holds**:
   - Dramatic blackout holds, clean pauses before an introductory title, and spacious compositions are **vital breathing room**, not rendering dropouts.
3. **Contrast & Line Density Rhythm**:
   - High-contrast wireframe density signifies technical precision and analytical complexity; open negative space signifies luxury, clarity, and contemplation. Alternating between dense technical detail and spacious minimalism is the core rhythm of premium motion design.
4. **Legibility & Reading Comfort**:
   - Even in fast-paced commercial sequences, hero copy must respect human reading speeds ($> 200\text{–}250\,\text{WPM}$). Never sacrifice comprehension for superficial speed.
