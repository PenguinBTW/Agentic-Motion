## 2. Pacing & Aesthetic Intent
- Snaps on musical beats are **intentional artistic accents** (e.g. sharp camera cuts or jump pans on kicks/downbeats). They must not be smoothed out into floaty drift.
- Blank blackout holds (such as the intro before the first beat or dramatic holds) are **intentional negative space**, not rendering dropouts.
- High-contrast wireframe line density reflects complex analytical thought; low line density reflects contemplation. Variety across scenes is essential.
